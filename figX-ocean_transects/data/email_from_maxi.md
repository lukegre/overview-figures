Below is some information that may be useful for you.

Sam also agrees that using the 2024 dataset instead of the 2023 dataset would be preferable. A few advantages are:

-The two CTD devices were crosschecked a their T and S distributions agreed well. 
-I interpolated the CTD data at 1 m intervals and then appended the nutrient data. 
-I checked that the overall distribution of each variable seems reasonable (which is not the case for the 2023 dataset). 

A few disadvantages:
-Due to the dense  ice melange in 2024, we could not get as close to the glacier terminus as we did in 2023. 
-The fluorescence sensor was not calibrated, so we cannot report absolute chlorophyll-a concentrations (X mg/l). However, we can still make relative comparisons between fjords, which was the main goal of the ocean cluster. 

For the plots, I would suggest showing S (perhaphs Tpot too), Nitrate (most clear evidence of upwelling, uM), and fluorescence (most clear indication of primary production). 

Also in attach the two separate scripts I use for land and marine sections. I use distance on the  x-axis, increasing from the first/innermost station towards the fjord mouth.  Please feel free to adapt them as needed.

You will also need another excel file (attached) including interpolated depths and distances of every station relative to the innermost part of the fjord.  The depths were extracted and interpolated from the bathymetry raster. I am sure this could be improved, so if you feel like refining it, I’ve also attached the bathymetry raster file.
